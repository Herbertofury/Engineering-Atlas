## What should be tested first

The most useful direction is to remove measured work, make necessary work cheaper, and keep resource ownership explicit. The following order is an editorial judgment for application and game-tooling research. The actual target's current implementation is the first control; a missing prerequisite can change the order.

| Order | Decision and concrete candidates | First falsifying case | Sources |

## Fourteen additional paper records

The new set comprises **12 full-text reviews and two explicitly limited reviews**. None of the artifacts was executed. Several works are recent preprints; venue year, submission date and journal publication are separate. The JPEG texture paper has a 2025 preprint lineage and 2026 journal publication. Full review depth and source links appear in the [consolidated watchlist](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Engineering-Atlas/blob/main/docs/paper-watchlist-2026.md#october-31-expansion-fourteen-additional-paper-records).

**Data and storage:** Tessera's October 29 preprint avoids intermediate join materialization for join-aggregates; its slowest baselines include single-run measurements. Tile-Based Decompression in Compiled GPU Engines explores lower device-memory traffic but preloads GPU-resident data and reports some regressions. RaBitQ-SSD requires recall-matched approximate retrieval and complete index-build accounting. RubikFS changes EROFS/Linux and uses an emulated embedded-oriented evaluation, so it is an immutable-image research lead. [IO31039](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40620v1) · [IO31042](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32695v1) · [IO31044](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02652v1) · [IO31047](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast26/presentation/huang)

**Build and browser execution:** the mold paper supplies a historical linker experiment and artifact protocol, not an October 3.0 performance claim. ParaWeb explores common parallel patterns across worker/GPU backends, with constrained data shapes, serialized self-contained functions, CPU/GPU precision differences and transfer costs. The Unseen Delta is an abstract-only compiler-diagnosis lead; no detailed mechanism or numbers were imported. [RT31029](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23228v1) · [RT31031](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19935) · [RT31030](https://conf.researchr.org/details/issta-2026/issta-2026-research-papers/73/The-Unseen-Delta-Characterizing-the-Compiler-Optimization-Landscape-via-Top-Down-Dif)

**Graphics and simulation:** The Essence of Entity Component System offers access/ownership ideas from a narrow case study. Variable-Rate Texture Compression demonstrates diffuse RGB textures and distinguishes pipeline timings that exclude CUDA/OpenGL transition overhead from teaser FPS that includes it. CuRast's opaque CUDA geometry and modified comparison scenes do not establish a general renderer replacement. Adaptive Bezier subdivision was reviewed through the author article and artifact because the full PDF exceeded retrieval limits; hardware Work Graphs and its fallback remain separate configurations. [GP31034](https://boyang.cs.uwm.edu/publication/sac2026_ECS.pdf) · [GP31036](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cgf.70338) · [GP31039](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21749v2) · [GP31041](https://gpuopen.com/learn/gpu-view-adaptive-subdivision/)

**Diagnosis and effect control:** TypeCraft attributes accesses to types/fields with x86 and debug-information constraints. The gigiprofiler paper's artifact is named OmniProfiler and introduces source/documentation/LLM/toolchain dependencies that must be pinned. Semisolates/Try can inspect effects of already-trusted commands; upstream explicitly says it is not an untrusted-command sandbox and allows network effects by default. Selectively applying files does not guarantee cross-file invariants or rollback of remote effects. ## Useful implementation choices, with boundaries

The expanded [performance guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Engineering-Atlas/blob/main/docs/performance.md#apply-current-methods-at-the-measured-bottleneck) includes concrete scheduling, worker ownership and containment examples plus build/measurement migration guidance. Examples are proposed implementation patterns, not executed demonstrations of a speedup.

**Count bytes and ownership, not only workers.** A bounded pool can still retain excessive memory when every job is enqueued eagerly with its full input. Admission must account for queued bytes, running buffers, retry ownership and output delivery. Tokio blocking closures already running cannot be aborted; Piscina cancellation terminates its worker and does not reconstruct transferred data. Cancellation semantics therefore belong in each runtime's separate test configuration. [IO31012](https://docs.rs/tokio/latest/tokio/task/fn.spawn_blocking.html) · [RT31010](https://github.com/piscinajs/piscina)

**Keep validation inside the measured representation path.** rkyv's checked access and FlatBuffers verification are part of the contract for untrusted or persisted input. A parser's selective-access result must not imply that every required field was checked. xxHash is a non-cryptographic fingerprint and does not replace content authentication; retain the update trust boundary. [IO31006](https://docs.rs/rkyv/latest/rkyv/) · [IO31008](https://flatbuffers.dev/languages/cpp/) · [IO31001](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson) · [IO31028](https://github.com/Cyan4973/xxHash)

**Separate observed progress from durable completion.** TanStack DB's optimistic state, confirmed synchronization and durable storage are different milestones. PGlite's documented IndexedDB VFS loads database files into memory on startup and coordinates tabs through a leader; test cold open, quota, memory and leader loss before choosing it for large browser catalogs. Automerge's merge behavior and storage growth need workload-specific validation. [RT31018](https://github.com/TanStack/db/blob/main/docs/overview.md) · [RT31019](https://github.com/TanStack/db/blob/main/README.md) · [IO31019](https://pglite.dev/docs/filesystems) · [IO31020](https://pglite.dev/docs/multi-tab-worker) · [IO31031](https://automerge.org/blog/automerge-3/)

**Make the frame and asset contract explicit.** SDL3 GPU and Slang address API/shader portability, Flecs/EnTT address entity storage and queries, Jolt addresses physics, and Taskflow/enkiTS address scheduling. Their contributions compose; they are not interchangeable entries in one speed ranking. Texture codecs, KTX containers, mesh transforms and animation sampling likewise need independent fidelity controls. [GP31001](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/releases/tag/release-3.4.18) · [GP31003](https://github.com/shader-slang/slang/releases/tag/v2026.20) · [GP31006](https://github.com/SanderMertens/flecs/releases/tag/v4.1.6) · [GP31008](https://github.com/skypjack/entt/releases/tag/v4.0.0) · [GP31010](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/releases/tag/v5.6.0) · [GP31012](https://github.com/taskflow/taskflow/releases/tag/v4.1.0) · [GP31014](https://github.com/dougbinks/enkiTS/releases/tag/v1.12) · [GP31020](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/KTX-Software/releases/tag/v4.4.2)

The existing Explorer now exposes **38 structured planned comparisons** from a single JSON registry. Selecting a plan filters the candidates and shows its decision, controls, gates, metrics and protocol. ## Retrieval limits and reproducibility

- The Unseen Delta remains abstract/metadata-only; adaptive Bezier remains an author-article/artifact review. No claim of full-paper review is made for either.
- ParaWeb's full text was recovered, but its linked artifact could not be retrieved. RubikFS's artifact was not retrieved. Their build/license/reproduction questions remain open.
- VideoLAN canonical GitLab retrieval was restricted; official project pages and official mirrors supplied the inspected evidence. No access restriction was bypassed.
- libmdbx identifies SourceCraft as upstream and distinguishes stable 0.14.x from development 0.15.x. A technical tag is not a stable release. No suitable additional Codeberg/sourcehut candidate was verified merely to fill a forge quota.
- rkyv's documentation and GitHub release endpoint show different versions. The record preserves that discrepancy without fabricating a package release date. Rolling documentation for LLVM and other tools must be matched to the eventual tested version.
- Existing source records and dated experiments are preserved. The new sources live in four indexed additive fragments; consumers must use the complete register.

Software validation checks the catalog, sources, proposed-plan contracts, deterministic generated assets, ranking/export logic and the public Explorer. It establishes that this contribution behaves as documented; it does not establish that catalog candidates are faster. Exact executed checks and publication evidence are retained in the [implementation validation record](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Engineering-Atlas/blob/main/experiments/research-explorer-validation/README.md).

## Evidence design decisions

**Popularity is a discovery signal.** Real GitHub API snapshots preserve observed counts, canonical repository URLs, archive state and license metadata. API errors are retained; unknown counts never become zero. A redirected React repository was resolved through the official API instead of inventing missing metadata. Counts can change after the observation.

**Community reporting is operationally separate.** A public issue form captures the candidate, exact track, version and experience. Approved reports are bound to the current issue-body hash and a verified maintainer, then deduplicated by stable account ID. The static ranking needs a complete refresh and a reviewed data/build update. It starts with zero reports, not seeded praise.

**External scores belong to full configurations.** The two imported cohorts are SWE-Bench Pro V2 Full and ProgramBench. Their task sets, scoring definitions and budget constraints remain separate. The imported eight observations are a documented subset; they do not stand for complete source coverage or an Atlas-run result.

**Local results come from retained raw data.** The builder imports 27 observations from the existing October 26 JSON experiment and checks medians, workload hashes, raw sample counts and the immutable evidence hash. It does not rerun the benchmark, erase semantic disqualifiers, or promote a microbenchmark to target acceptance.

**Year precision stays honest.** Some 2026 venue papers publish only a year or month in the reviewed material. Those records carry a supported publication year and a null exact date. Year filtering includes them; exact-date ordering does not invent January 1.

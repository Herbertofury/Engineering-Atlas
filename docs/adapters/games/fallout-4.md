## Decision and useful discovery routes

A Fallout 4 adapter should follow creator identity across original sites, mod hosts, translated publications and community posts while retaining the role at each hop. The strongest useful additions are small technical forums (AFK/TES Alliance), dedicated project communities (Sim Settlements, London, Miami, Cascadia and Capital Wasteland), and original creator hubs (Kinggath, CDante, SKK and OddLittleTurtle). Their value is original release/support context and provenance, not a claim that every site hosts a downloadable archive. Regional publication sites add language and localization provenance. MRC and The Midnight Ride are discovery/reference surfaces; they must not inflate binary-host counts.

The rejected approach is to treat an indexed title, host landing page or profile avatar as a complete release extraction. Direct inspection exposed current failures that an undifferentiated host list hides: Creations/3DM shells, GUN login, AFK profile 403, incorrect Sim thread resolution and a stale Creative Family destination. Failed requests remain evidence instead of being replaced by search snippets.
